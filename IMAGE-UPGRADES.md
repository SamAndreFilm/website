# Images that need a higher-resolution source

Every image on the site was taken from the WordPress export of the old
samandre.com. Covers are exported at up to **2560 px** on the long edge
(they are used full-bleed as the hero on each project page) and gallery
stills at up to **2000 px**. Where a source file is smaller than that, the
site is showing everything the file has — the shortfall is in the source,
not the build.

This is the list of what would benefit from a better original.

## Reference points

| Use | Wanted | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Grid tile | 1400–1920 px long edge | tile displays ~700–960 px wide, doubled for retina |
| Full-bleed hero | 2400–2560 px long edge | covers a 1280 px layout at 2x pixel density |
| Press kit / festival still | 2400x3000 px (300 DPI at 8x10 in) | standard print request |

## Why the film stills are smaller than the photography

The film stills have a median long edge of 1920 px — they are frame grabs
from 1080p masters, and 1920x1080 is the ceiling of an HD frame. The
photography sits at a median of 3960 px because it comes off a stills
camera. Two things close that gap on future projects:

- Pull stills from the highest-resolution master available. A grab from
  4K UHD is 8.3 MP against 2.1 MP from 1080p, and clears the press-kit bar.
- Shoot dedicated unit stills on a stills body during production. That is
  what publicists and festivals ask for, and it is why unit photography is
  normally 24–45 MP.
